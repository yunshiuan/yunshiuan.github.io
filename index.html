01 / Research

From human behavior to dependable agent behavior.

I develop methods that make language-model agents more capable and better grounded in what matters: completing multi-step tasks, reflecting diverse human beliefs, and interacting reliably in the real world.

My research draws on machine learning, cognitive science, and empirical social simulation. I am particularly interested in the evaluation and post-training loops that turn promising models into useful agents.

  1. 01

    Train

    Reinforcement learning and preference optimization for interactive, tool-using agents.

  2. 02

    Evaluate

    Scalable methods for measuring multi-turn reasoning, tool use, and task completion.

  3. 03

    Align

    Human-centered multi-agent systems that represent diverse beliefs and social dynamics.
